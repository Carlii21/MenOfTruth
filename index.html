WHO WE ARE

Our Vision

To build strong, responsible men who lead with truth, integrity, courage and purpose, while promoting mental heath, emotional strength and positive well-being in men and boys.

Our Goals

Build strong and responsible men.

Promote good mental health and emotional well-being.

Encourage unity among men and boys.

Strengthen families and relationships.

Develop leadership and positive character.

Encourage men to support and mentor one another.

Create opportunities for personal and community development.

Our Objectives

Encourage honesty, discipline, respect and responsibility.

Create a safe space where men can speak openly about their challenges.

Promote mental, emotional and social well-being.

Equip men with leadership and life skills.

Support boys as they grow into responsible men.

Promote teamwork, brotherhood and health relationships.

Help men discover and develop their talents.

Serve and uplift our communities.

Our Motto

Qina Ndoda, Khuluma Ndoda -- Strong Men, Truthful Men.
